5The intake questions

Read him the questions the desk asks. Are these the ones he wants asked, in this order, in this wording?

  • Any question he would remove?
  • Any he would add — bearing in mind nothing about menstruation, pregnancy, mental health, alcohol or home life is ever asked at a front desk?
  • Any wording that is wrong in Bangla?

6The consultation and the prescription

Let him type one. Then confirm it, and show him that the record locks and an amendment is recorded rather than a rewrite.

7Twenty seconds, honestly

Not "do you like it". Ask: on a Tuesday evening with forty patients waiting, is this faster than the file you keep now, or slower?

The three things only he can do. Nothing real happens until these are done, and nobody else can do them. Get a date against each one before you leave the room.
